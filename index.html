A PERSONAL NOTE

Built by curiosity.
Driven by purpose.

I’m Hasindu Himal Pemathilaka, an electrical engineer from the coastal city of Galle, Sri Lanka, now pursuing a thesis-based Master’s degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Alberta.

My journey has taken me from a first-class honours degree at the University of Moratuwa to teaching future engineers and exploring the challenges of modern power systems. I believe that good engineering should make a difference beyond the drawing board.

ROLLING SPHERE METHOD ↗ BINARY INTEGER PROGRAMMING
CAPSTONE · JUL 2025AWARD WINNING PROJECT

Smarter lightning
protection design.

Developed a 3D software tool that optimizes the positioning of air terminals and conductors to protect complex buildings—combining geometric modelling with binary integer linear programming.

DISCOVER THE APPROACH +

Applied the Rolling Sphere Method to detect exposed regions, optimized protection configurations, and validated the results on simulated 3D building models against conventional design approaches.

Detecting faults
before they escalate.
